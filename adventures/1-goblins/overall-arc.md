Starts with goblin raids of increasing quantity. There is a goblin king who is going to take over part of the horseshoe. Including most of the high marches, as well as parts of northern greenwold and western lowcester.

This arc will be the rise of the goblin from small tribes to this huge army. Eventually there will be a huge battle with the ducal armies. 

Outcome can go either way, because at the end, the goblin shamans raise all the dead, human & goblin alike, to overthrow the goblins. This leads to 2-undead.md.

Goblin Army (tribal)

Size and scope
- Total warhost: roughly 900,000 to 1,100,000 goblins at peak.
- Not a standing army; it is a coalition of tribes that swells after victories.
- Raiding bands are typically 30 to 80; warbands are 200 to 600; a full muster is 20,000 to 80,000.
- Composition: 10% worg cavalry, 10% ranged units, 80% infantry.

Structure
- Goblin King (warlord of warlords): the single voice over the full host.
- Tribes: 200+ tribes of varying size (roughly 500 to 12,000 each).
- Clans: each tribe breaks into 3 to 9 clans that share a totem and camp.
- Warbands: clan-sized units led by a spear-boss (200 to 600 each).
- Raid packs: small crews for livestock theft, arson, and scouting (30 to 80).
- Shaman circles: each tribe has 1 to 3 shamans who keep rites and war-omens.

Roles and special groups
- Screamers: runners and signalers who coordinate ambushes.
- Breakers: strongest goblins with crude shields and heavy clubs.
- Skulkers: night raiders and sappers; experts at smoke and confusion.
- Beast-tenders: handle worgs, boar-guard, and stolen horses.
- Binder shamans: ritual leaders who negotiate alliances and enforce taboo.
