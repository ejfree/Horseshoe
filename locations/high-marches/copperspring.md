---
title: Copperspring
slug: copperspring
---

Overview
- A market village clustered around a mineral spring that runs green-blue.
- The spring feeds shallow copper pits and small smelter sheds.

Population
- ~1,000 within palisade; ~400 in pit shacks and spring huts.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, on a spur road between Ironpool and Granite Steps.
- Approaches: cart track to Redspan; ridge path to Ridgeford.

Economy
- Copper ore, small smelts, and spring water drawn for trade.
- Repair services for pit tools and wagon bands.

Authority & Order
- Civic: Spring Council led by a village reeve.
- Church: small Bond Hall for pit claims and water rights.

Defenses
- Timber palisade with a locked spring gate.
- Watch posts at the pit lanes.

Notables
- Spring Keeper Nessa Vorn — protective of the water, mistrusts outsiders.
- Pit Boss Orik Vale — keeps a tight grip on ore shares.
- Reeve Lira Dent — fair broker in disputes.

Landmarks
- Copper Spring: mineral pool with stained stone rims.
- Smelt Sheds: low roofs with steady smoke.
- Pit Lane: adits and ore ramps carved into the slope.

Key Locations
- Assay Hut: scales and sample racks.
- Bathhouse: warm spring room used for meetings.
- Tool Yard: hammer racks and spare picks.

Threats & Pressures
- The spring runs low in dry years.
- Poisoned runoff threatens downstream crops.

Adventure Hooks
- A new ore vein sparks a claim fight.
- Someone taints the spring on a market day.
- A pit collapse seals a rich pocket.

Notes
- Copperspring relies on clean water; protect the spring and the village thrives.
