---
title: Fir Hollow
slug: fir-hollow
---

Overview
- A market village tucked into a fir-lined hollow, known for herbs and resin.
- Charcoal rings smolder at the edge of the trees.

Population
- ~900 within palisade; ~300 in hunter huts and resin camps.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, along a forest track between Blackpine and Cliffmoot.
- Approaches: narrow timber road and a foot trail to ridge camps.

Economy
- Resin, herbs, and small game trade.
- Charcoal and firewood for nearby towns.

Authority & Order
- Civic: Forester Steward and a village reeve.
- Church: small Bond Hall for herb contracts and hunting bonds.

Defenses
- Thorn fence, watch posts, and ranger patrols nearby.
- Firebreaks cut around the village edge.

Notables
- Steward Elen Hart — quiet, strict about forest rules.
- Herbwife Pira Glen — knows every useful plant in the hollow.
- Hunter Kess Rowan — guides travelers and tracks trouble.

Landmarks
- Fir Hollow Grove: old firs marked with oath ribbons.
- Herb Yard: drying racks and covered benches.
- Charcoal Rings: low mounds that burn for days.

Key Locations
- Foresters' Shed: maps of the hollow and cutting quotas.
- Market Green: small stalls and a posted rates board.
- Hunters' Lodge: bunks, skins, and trail notes.

Threats & Pressures
- Wolves push closer in harsh winters.
- Blight patches in the firs threaten resin yields.

Adventure Hooks
- Hunters vanish on the ridge trail.
- A blight spreads with unnatural speed.
- A trader claims to have found a hidden ruin in the hollow.

Notes
- Fir Hollow survives by knowing the forest and keeping it healthy.
