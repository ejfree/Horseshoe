---
title: Sawyergate
slug: sawyergate
---

Overview
- A city grown around a fortified timber gate where the upland cuts meet the lowland roads.
- Every log, beam, and plank leaving the High Marches is tallied here.

Population
- ~12,000 within walls; ~6,000 in mill camps and river huts.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, lower ridge above the river flats.
- Approaches: timber road from Blackpine; cart road to Sluicebend.

Economy
- Sawmills, lumber yards, cartwrights, and ropewalks.
- Gate tolls and inspection fees on outbound timber.

Authority & Order
- Civic: Gate Council of mill masters and a city reeve.
- Church: Bond Hall that seals timber brands and shipping contracts.

Defenses
- Fortified gatehouse, boom across the river, and patrols along the log runs.
- Alarm horns at the mills and on the river stairs.

Notables
- Gate Reeve Rolan Vick — keeps the tallies clean and the queue moving.
- Sawmistress Jana Pike — runs the biggest mill and hires hard.
- River Warden Lysa Dorr — enforces the boom and river rules.

Landmarks
- The Gate: heavy timber portal with iron bands and a toll hall.
- Mill Row: long sheds of saws and whirring belts.
- Log Boom: chained logs that control river traffic.

Key Locations
- Tally House: brands, weights, and shipment seals.
- Cartwright Lane: wagon frames, wheels, and repair yards.
- River Stairs: loading steps for barge trains.

Threats & Pressures
- Spring floods can tear the boom and wreck yards.
- Illegal cutters slip unmarked logs into the stream.

Adventure Hooks
- A mill fire exposes a stash of stolen brands.
- The boom is sabotaged on a busy export day.
- A cartwright swears a rival is using cursed wood.

Notes
- Sawyergate keeps the timber economy honest, or tries to.
