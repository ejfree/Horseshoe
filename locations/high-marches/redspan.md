title: Redspan
slug: redspan
---

Overview
- A mining city built around a massive ironstone bridge that crosses a sheer red gorge.
- The span is both road and fortress, with smelter smoke rising from its flanks.

Population
- ~40,000 within walls; ~10,000 in mining camps and slag yards.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, on the Iron Road between Stonecross and the Redspan Mines.
- Approaches: cliff road to quarries; switchbacks down to ore adits.

Economy
- Iron and copper extraction, ore grading, and bar smelting.
- Bridge tolls and hauling contracts for the Iron Road.

Authority & Order
- Civic: mine stewards under a city reeve, advised by a bridge captain.
- Church: Bond Hall focused on contracts, weights, and scale disputes.

Defenses
- Fortified bridge towers, gatehouses, and reinforced ore hoists.
- Alarm bells at adit mouths and militia musters in the span yards.

Notables
- Bridge Captain Sarra Pell — stern guardian of the span and its chains.
- Mine Steward Vorn Bale — methodical, hated by unsafe crews.
- Bondwright Clerk Tamsin Reed — settles disputes with a steady hand.
- Furnace Master Irel Hask — knows the secrets of the hottest blooms.

Landmarks
- Redspan Bridge: ironstone arch with guard towers and toll booths.
- Smelter Row: a canyon of furnaces and slag pits.
- Ore Plaza: open yard where sacks are graded and sealed.

Key Locations
- Span Barracks: watch, training yard, and signal bells.
- Assay House: scales, acids, and a locked seal vault.
- Quarry Lifts: timber cranes that haul stone blocks and ore.
- Slag Gardens: cooling mounds used as windbreaks and meeting spots.

Threats & Pressures
- Bridge strain from heavy caravans and spring quakes.
- Gas pockets in deep adits force periodic shutdowns.

Adventure Hooks
- A survey team disappears in a new red seam.
- Rival stewards falsify weights to steal a contract.
- The bridge chain snaps during a storm and someone is blamed.

Notes
- Redspan is a work city: loud, strict, and always short on clean water.
