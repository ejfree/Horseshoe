title: Oath-Hall Borough
slug: oath-hall-borough
---

Overview
- A legal city built around the great Oath-Hall, where clans swear their largest bonds.
- Inns, seal shops, and scribes crowd the ring roads.

Population
- ~18,000 within walls; ~4,000 in surrounding oath villages.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, near the Oath-Hall of Clans.
- Approaches: ridge road from Stonecross; valley road to Pinebridge.

Economy
- Arbitration services, sealcraft, record keeping, and hosting.
- Markets for justice fees, witnesses, and trusted escorts.

Authority & Order
- Civic: Oath judges and a ring council.
- Church: major Bond Hall with an extensive standing book archive.

Defenses
- Ring wall, warden towers, and strict gate searches.
- City levy trained to protect witnesses and archives.

Notables
- First Bondwright Selma Jett — exacting, careful with the seals.
- Ring Warden Hoss Vale — loyal to the oath ring, suspicious of outsiders.
- Scribe Master Nerin Pike — keeps the archive index and knows all names.

Landmarks
- The Oath-Hall: vast ringed chamber and witness benches.
- Witness Towers: small towers for sworn guardians.
- Seal Market: stalls of wax, inks, and crafted stamps.

Key Locations
- Archive Vaults: stone chambers holding standing books.
- Oath Yard: open court for public hearings.
- Quiet Inns: hostels that enforce silence for negotiators.

Threats & Pressures
- Feud parties attempt to sway judges or seize records.
- Forged seals circulate during large clan disputes.

Adventure Hooks
- An oath ledger goes missing on the eve of a trial.
- A witness is threatened and seeks hidden escort.
- A judge is accused of breaking the oath ring.

Notes
- Oath-Hall Borough is the duchy’s conscience and its busiest courtroom.
