---
title: Granite Steps
slug: granite-steps
---

Overview
- A terraced city built into a stepped ridge, famous for its stonework and cliff gardens.
- Water runs in channels down each level, powering lifts and mills.

Population
- ~15,000 within walls; ~5,000 in quarry villages and step farms.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, on a ridge road between Stonecross and Sawyergate.
- Approaches: stair roads up the ridge; quarry lanes along the lower steps.

Economy
- Stone quarrying, masonry, and finished blocks for Iron Road works.
- Cistern management and irrigation goods for terrace farms.

Authority & Order
- Civic: Guild of Masons and a Stairwarden council.
- Church: Bond Hall that tracks contracts for stone and water rights.

Defenses
- Tiered walls, gated stairwells, and rockfall barriers.
- Watch posts at each step and a trained levy of stoneworkers.

Notables
- Stairwarden Ilse Korr — patient, strict, and hard to bribe.
- Master Mason Hadrik Stone — proud of his perfect cuts.
- Cistern Keeper Vale Len — watches the water and the rumors.

Landmarks
- The Great Steps: main ascent lined with carved standing stones.
- Sky Cistern: highest reservoir with a view across the duchy.
- Quarry Mouths: tiered adits with timber hoists.

Key Locations
- Stone Market: block yard with size marks and seal stamps.
- Lift House: pulley lifts for cargo and tools.
- Terrace Gardens: stepped plots with windbreak walls.

Threats & Pressures
- Rockslides threaten lower terraces in wet seasons.
- Water disputes between farms and quarry crews.

Adventure Hooks
- A crack opens on the Great Steps after a tremor.
- A load of dressed stone goes missing en route to Stonecross.
- A cistern is found tainted on the eve of a festival.

Notes
- Granite Steps is a crafts city; its reputation rests on clean cuts and steady water.
