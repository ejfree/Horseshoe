title: Ironpool
slug: ironpool
---

Overview
- A town clustered around a mineral pool that stains stone and tools red-brown.
- Bloomery stacks ring the water, and steam rises in the cold.

Population
- ~2,500 within walls; ~1,000 in pits and smelter camps.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, on a low ridge between Redspan and Granite Steps.
- Approaches: ore road to Redspan; cart track to Sawyergate.

Economy
- Bog iron gathering, bloomery smelting, and slag brick making.
- Tool repair and wagon ironwork for nearby camps.

Authority & Order
- Civic: Pit Council led by a town reeve.
- Church: Bond Hall that tracks ore claims and smelter debts.

Defenses
- Earthen berm, timber palisade, and watch posts by the pits.
- Militia of smelter crews with heavy hammers.

Notables
- Smelter Master Hobb Vail — knows every furnace draft by ear.
- Reeve Kera Noll — keeps the ore claims sorted and tempers low.
- Pit Boss Eren Koss — hard on safety, hated by shortcuts.

Landmarks
- Ironpool: mineral pool with rust-red edges.
- Slag Ridge: stacked bricks used as windbreaks.
- Bloomery Row: low furnaces with constant smoke.

Key Locations
- Assay Shed: scales and sample racks.
- Water Wheel: drives bellows and lifts.
- Pit Camp: tents, tool racks, and a tally post.

Threats & Pressures
- The pool runs low in dry seasons, slowing smelts.
- Tainted runoff threatens downstream farms.

Adventure Hooks
- A bloomery fire burns green, and no one knows why.
- A pit collapse traps a crew with a rich seam.
- The town is accused of poisoning a rival's water.

Notes
- Ironpool is small but vital; its iron keeps many forges fed.
