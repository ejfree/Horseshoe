title: Blackpine

Overview
- A timber city buried in the Blackpine Tracts, its roofs and yards dusted in resin.
- The air smells of sap, smoke, and wet bark from year-round cutting.

Population
- ~28,000 within walls; ~12,000 in cutters' camps and charcoal rings.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, deep in the Blackpine Tracts.
- Approaches: timber roads to Sawyergate; ridge path to Cliffmoot.

Economy
- Timber, resin, charcoal, and rough-cut beams.
- Sledge caravans haul logs to Sawyergate and Sluicebend.

Authority & Order
- Civic: Foresters' Council led by a city reeve.
- Church: Bond Hall that arbitrates timber claims and cutter contracts.

Defenses
- Stockade wall, ditch, and ranger patrols on the outer cuts.
- Firebreaks and watchtowers to spot blazes early.

Notables
- High Forester Olen Marr — calm, strict about cutting rules.
- Sawmaster Tera Vosk — runs the biggest mill, hates waste.
- Ranger Captain Pell Grey — keeps the patrols moving.

Landmarks
- The Resin Pools: shallow pits where sap is gathered and cooked.
- Sawmill Row: waterwheels and long sheds of saws.
- Old Oath Tree: ancient pine used for oath rings and warnings.

Key Locations
- Cutter Yard: hiring posts, scrip chits, and tool racks.
- Tar Kilns: smoky mounds that burn for days.
- Rangers' Hall: maps of the tracts and missing-camp boards.

Threats & Pressures
- Forest fires in dry seasons can cut off whole tracts.
- Predators and goblin scouts harry the outer camps.

Adventure Hooks
- A cutter crew vanishes beyond a new firebreak.
- Resin theft points to a hidden still in the woods.
- The Old Oath Tree is found scarred with fresh blade marks.

Notes
- Blackpine runs on schedules and quotas; a missed haul ripples across the duchy.
