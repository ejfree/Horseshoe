---
title: Ridgeford
slug: ridgeford
---

Overview
- A small town at a shallow ford where ridge traffic crosses a fast river.
- Drovers, scouts, and timber crews meet here to trade news.

Population
- ~3,000 within walls; ~1,500 in nearby hamlets and drover camps.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, on a ridge road between Pinebridge and Timberfall.
- Approaches: ford crossing and a stone-marked track along the ridge.

Economy
- Drover services, livestock markets, and small ferries in high water.
- Repair yards for wagons and harness.

Authority & Order
- Civic: town reeve and a drovers' guild.
- Church: small Bond Hall that keeps trade bonds and herd tallies.

Defenses
- Palisade with two gate-stiles and a ford watch.
- River stakes and horn posts for alarms.

Notables
- Reeve Mara Kett — patient, sharp-eyed, and fair.
- Drover Captain Jace Orin — knows every herd route in the marches.
- Ford Warden Tamm Rill — keeps the stakes and watch fires.

Landmarks
- Ridge Ford: marked by stone cairns and cut steps.
- Drover Green: open yard for herds and trade tents.
- Old Ford Shrine: small oath ring for safe crossings.

Key Locations
- Harness Yard: leatherworks and wagon repairs.
- River Camp: ferry ropes and drying racks.
- Watch Hut: horn, lanterns, and a warmed bench.

Threats & Pressures
- Spring floods can make the ford impassable for days.
- Goblin scouts test the ford at night.

Adventure Hooks
- A herd goes missing between Ridgeford and Pinebridge.
- The ford stakes are cut and swept away.
- A drover claims a rival is poisoning his stock.

Notes
- Ridgeford is small but strategic; all ridge traffic depends on the crossing.
