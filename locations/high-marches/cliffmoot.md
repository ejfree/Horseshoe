title: Cliffmoot
slug: cliffmoot
---

Overview
- A large town perched on a wide cliff shelf where clans meet to argue and trade.
- Wind whistles through the moot stones, carrying every word.

Population
- ~7,000 within walls; ~3,000 in goat camps and cliff farms.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, on a cliff road between Blackpine and Lantern Pass.
- Approaches: ridge path to the high passes; rope lift from the valley.

Economy
- Goat herding, cliff farming, and trade from clan gatherings.
- Stone cutting and ropework for lift rigs.

Authority & Order
- Civic: Moot Council led by a chosen speaker.
- Church: small Bond Hall for dispute records and trade bonds.

Defenses
- Cliffside palisade, rope lift controls, and a watch ledge.
- Stones are piled for defensive drops along the rim.

Notables
- Moot Speaker Adra Kyn — sharp-tongued, respected by the clans.
- Rope Master Bram Kett — keeps the lift safe and the fees paid.
- Herdmistress Olla Fen — knows every ridge herd path.

Landmarks
- Moot Stone Circle: ring of stones where oaths and debates are heard.
- Cliff Walk: narrow ledge used for patrols and announcements.
- Wind Stables: sheltered pens for pack goats.

Key Locations
- Market Ledge: stalls secured with stone anchors and ropes.
- Rope Lift House: winches, brakes, and spare lines.
- Hearth Hall: communal lodge for visiting clans.

Threats & Pressures
- Rockfalls threaten the rope lift and lower farms.
- Clan feuds can turn the moot into a brawl.

Adventure Hooks
- A moot banner is stolen on the day of talks.
- The rope lift jammed with a cargo no one claims.
- A herd vanishes beyond a known ridge line.

Notes
- Cliffmoot is neutral ground until it is not.
