---
title: Timberfall
slug: timberfall
---

Overview
- A town built beside a tall cascade where log drives are broken and sorted.
- The falls are loud and constant, and the boom crews never stop.

Population
- ~4,000 within walls; ~2,000 in log camps and mills.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, on the main river between Ridgeford and Sluicebend.
- Approaches: river barges and the ridge road along the falls.

Economy
- Log sorting, sawmills, and timber rafting services.
- Pitch, tar, and rope for river work.

Authority & Order
- Civic: Log Captains' Council and a town reeve.
- Church: Bond Hall that seals timber marks and rafting contracts.

Defenses
- River boom, watch towers, and a fortified mill yard.
- Water barriers can be dropped to stall raiders.

Notables
- Boom Captain Ressa Hark — quick, loud, and reliable.
- Millmaster Garen Voss — runs the largest sawshed.
- River Warden Tia Pell — keeps the boom and the ladders safe.

Landmarks
- Timberfall Cascade: tall falls with a misty spray.
- Boom Yard: chained logs and winches at the river bend.
- Mill Row: waterwheels and saws in constant motion.

Key Locations
- Log Stairs: stone steps for crew access and loading.
- Pitch House: tar vats and rope racks.
- Raft Camp: bunks and repair sheds for crews.

Threats & Pressures
- Log jams can dam the river and flood the lower yards.
- Rival crews fight over prime runs.

Adventure Hooks
- A raft train disappears between Timberfall and Sluicebend.
- The boom is cut the night before a major run.
- A mill worker claims a water spirit is angry.

Notes
- Timberfall is loud, wet, and essential to the timber economy.
