title: Pinebridge

---

Overview
- A large town built around a long timber bridge over a fast, cold river.
- The bridge is its lifeline, and every family has a stake in its upkeep.

Population
- ~6,000 within walls; ~2,000 in ferries and riverside farms.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, on the main river below Granite Steps.
- Approaches: ridge road to Oath-Hall Borough; river road to Sluicebend.

Economy
- Bridge tolls, resin trade, and river barges.
- Fish weirs and ferry contracts in high season.

Authority & Order
- Civic: Bridge Wardens and a town reeve.
- Church: Bond Hall that tracks toll exemptions and ferry bonds.

Defenses
- Bridge fort with gatehouse and a chain boom upstream.
- Patrols on both banks and watch horns at the tollhouse.

Notables
- Bridge Reeve Corin Hale — honest, stubborn, and tired of repairs.
- Ferrymaster Lysa Grell — knows the currents and every lie told on them.
- Warden Kess Dorne — keeps the boom and the gate tight.

Landmarks
- Pinebridge Span: long timber bridge with iron bands.
- Tollhouse: stone block with a posted rates board.
- Resin Yards: vats and racks along the riverbank.

Key Locations
- Ferry Stairs: broad steps for river crossings and loading.
- Repair Yard: spare beams, iron dogs, and pulley rigs.
- River Shrine: small oath ring for safe passage.

Threats & Pressures
- Floods and ice threaten the bridge each winter.
- Smugglers try to bypass tolls by night.

Adventure Hooks
- The bridge creaks with a new crack no one can explain.
- A ferry crew vanishes on the foggy stretch below town.
- Toll records show a week of missing entries.

Notes
- Pinebridge is a town of carpenters, ferrymen, and watchful wardens.
