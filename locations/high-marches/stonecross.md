title: Stonecross

---

Overview
- Seat of House Ironholt, a fortress-city where two ridgelines meet and the Iron Road bends through a high saddle.
- Smoke from forges hangs above slate roofs, and caravan bells mark the city’s steady pulse.

Population
- ~70,000 within walls; another ~20,000 in outer camps, quarries, and road holds.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, on the Iron Road between Redspan and Lantern Pass.
- Approaches: east ridge road to the low duchies; west switchbacks to the timber tracts.

Economy
- Iron and copper smelting, toolmaking, and caravan outfitting.
- Toll rights on the Iron Road and storage fees in the caravan yards.

Authority & Order
- Civic: ducal court, oath-ring council, and the Iron Road master of caravans.
- Church: major Bond Hall and standing book archive for the highland clans.

Defenses
- Granite curtain walls, cliffside keeps, and gatehouses with chain lifts.
- Signal towers on both ridgelines and a drilled city levy.

Notables
- Duke Torvin Ironholt — taciturn ruler, favors long planning over quick strikes.
- Lady Brinna of Lantern Pass — famed hunter and mediator between clans.
- Bram Ironholt — heir apparent, studying safer mining methods and modern works.
- Iron Road Master Heddan — keeps caravans moving and debts settled.

Landmarks
- Oath-Hall of Clans: ringed hall where major accords are sealed.
- The Anvil Stair: a switchbacked stone ascent carved into the ridge.
- Iron Road Gate: main caravan gate and tally plaza.

Key Locations
- Ducal Keep: high towered seat overlooking the road.
- Forge Row: heat-hazed lane of smithies and foundries.
- Caravan Yards: stables, ledgers, and guarded storehouses.
- Stonecross Market: weight stones, salt vaults, and coin exchangers.

Threats & Pressures
- Pass closures in hard winter strain food stores.
- Clan disputes over tolls threaten the oath-ring.

Adventure Hooks
- A caravan vanishes on the Iron Road with an oath-sealed cargo.
- A forge fire reveals strange slag that no smith can name.
- A clan accuses another of tampering with the pass signals.

Notes
- Stonecross anchors House Ironholt’s authority and sets the tone for High Marches law.
