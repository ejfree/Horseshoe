---
title: Lantern Pass
slug: lantern-pass
---

Overview
- High pass city known for its watchfires and signal lamps that guide caravans through fog and snow.
- Built around a wind-scoured saddle where the ridges open to the northern routes.

Population
- ~35,000 within walls; another ~5,000 in guide lodges and relay huts.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, highest maintained pass on the Iron Road.
- Approaches: switchbacks from Stonecross, ridge trail toward northern clans.

Economy
- Toll rights, guide services, lamp oil, and pass maintenance contracts.
- Winter storage and mule teams for caravan relays.

Authority & Order
- Civic: Pass Wardens and a council of ridge clans.
- Church: Bond Hall that records toll exemptions and rescue debts.

Defenses
- Watchfire towers, avalanche berms, and a stone gate set into the saddle.
- Storm bells and shutter signals for emergency closures.

Notables
- Warden Karra Vell — keeps the gates, hates delays.
- Signal Master Joss Rake — manages beacon crews and storm logs.
- Guide Captain Mira Thane — runs the mule teams and rescue drills.

Landmarks
- The Lantern Stair: terraced ascent lined with windbreak walls.
- Pass Gate: heavy stone gate with a toll hall.
- Beacon Ridge: highest tower with a far-seen lamp.

Key Locations
- Warden Hall: maps, storm logs, and rescue gear.
- Mule Yards: stables, feed lofts, and repair sheds.
- Ridge Shrine: small oath ring for travelers and guides.

Threats & Pressures
- Whiteout storms can trap caravans for weeks.
- Raiders cut guide ropes and steal lamp oil.

Adventure Hooks
- A beacon fails to light and the wardens suspect sabotage.
- A rescue team returns without its guide captain.
- A clan demands toll exemptions for a claimed ancestral right.

Notes
- Lantern Pass is the safety valve of the Iron Road; it closes rarely but decisively.
