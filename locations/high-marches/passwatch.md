title: Passwatch
slug: passwatch
---

Overview
- A market village built around a small watchtower that oversees a minor pass.
- Guides, muleteers, and patrols stop here before taking the narrow ridge trail.

Population
- ~1,200 within palisade; ~300 in nearby ridge huts.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, on a minor ridge pass off the Iron Road.
- Approaches: ridge trail to Lantern Pass; cart track to Anvil Stair Market.

Economy
- Guide services, tolls, and provisions for pass traffic.
- Small trade in lamp oil, rope, and winter gear.

Authority & Order
- Civic: Watch Captain and a village reeve.
- Church: small Bond Hall for toll receipts and rescue debts.

Defenses
- Stone watchtower with a signal brazier.
- Palisade and a gate-stile with a horn post.

Notables
- Watch Captain Dena Holt — sharp-eyed, wary of strangers.
- Reeve Kell Marr — keeps stores tight and prices fair.
- Guide Soren Pike — knows hidden trails and safer ledges.

Landmarks
- Passwatch Tower: signal brazier and long view over the saddle.
- Guide Yard: posts with route notes and weather signs.
- Ridge Shrine: small oath ring for safe passage.

Key Locations
- Guesthouse: bunks and a common room for travelers.
- Mule Pens: feed loft and tack repair bench.
- Signal Cairns: stacked stones marking the trail.

Threats & Pressures
- Snow squalls cut the trail with little warning.
- Raiders target supply stores in lean months.

Adventure Hooks
- The signal fire fails to light during a storm.
- A guide returns alone with a broken rope.
- A toll cache is stolen from the tower.

Notes
- Passwatch is small but crucial for keeping minor routes open.
