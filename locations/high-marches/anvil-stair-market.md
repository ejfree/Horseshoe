title: Anvil Stair Market

Overview
- A large town clustered around the base of the Anvil Stair, where caravans regroup before the climb.
- Market stalls line the switchbacks, selling tools, tack, and mountain gear.

Population
- ~8,000 within walls; ~4,000 in caravan camps and stair keeps.

Location & Access
- Region: High Marches, at the lower end of the Anvil Stair.
- Approaches: Iron Road to Stonecross; ridge road to Granite Steps.

Economy
- Caravan services, smithing, and pack animal trade.
- Market tolls and storage fees for uphill cargo.

Authority & Order
- Civic: Market Reeve and a guild council of smiths and teamsters.
- Church: Bond Hall for caravan bonds and posted rates.

Defenses
- Stair garrison, stone gate, and patrol posts along the first switchbacks.
- Alarm bells that carry up the stair.

Notables
- Market Reeve Jorin Voss — efficient, hard on price fixers.
- Master Smith Hela Brant — runs the largest forge, fair but fierce.
- Stair Captain Rell Gage — keeps the stair clear in bad weather.

Landmarks
- The Anvil Stair: carved ascent with broad landings.
- Market Hall: covered stalls and a posted rates board.
- Iron Bell: alarm bell visible from the road.

Key Locations
- Caravan Yard: pens, feed stores, and contract boards.
- Smith Row: forges, tool racks, and iron sales.
- Teamster Lodge: bunks and a stable tax office.

Threats & Pressures
- Bandits on the lower stair target slow caravans.
- Shortages of feed during long winters.

Adventure Hooks
- A caravan master claims his cargo vanished inside the yard.
- A smith accuses rivals of using cursed ore.
- A storm traps a caravan and the stair captain asks for help.

Notes
- Anvil Stair Market is the last easy stop before the climb.
